Each of pi's 56 colours is a Tone, so an author paints in `mdQuote`, `toolDiffAdded` or `thinkingText`, and a palette made for pi has a place for each of its colours. A Tone's name is open, so a feature gives Tones of its own. A Style can be `like` another Tone, and follows it. A Tone in a Hue, the terminal's sixteen or `orange`, follows a layer's `hues`.
